## 5.1 What is ShareSquare?

ShareSquare is the place in UvA AI Chat where you share useful AI assets with other users and reuse what others have shared. You can share four types of assets: **Personas**, **Prompts**, **Projects** and **Extensions**. Have you built a persona that works well in your course, or a prompt you use every week? Through ShareSquare, others can copy it and get started right away. The other way round, you can take over an asset from a colleague or fellow student and adapt it to your own needs.

Open ShareSquare via the share icon <Icon name="Share2" color="black" size={20} /> (three connected dots) at the bottom of the row of icons in the left sidebar. At the top of every ShareSquare page you'll find a bar with four options:

| Option | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Explore** | Browse popular and recently shared assets (section 5.2) |
| **Search** | Search all shared assets (section 5.2) |
| **Your uploads** | Manage the assets you have shared (section 5.6) |
| **+ Add** | Share an asset of your own (section 5.5) |

**ShareSquare, Groups or sharing a chat?**

UvA AI Chat offers several ways to share your work. Choose the one that fits your purpose:

| Function | Shared with | What the other person gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ShareSquare | Other UvA AI Chat users | Their own copy, to use and adapt |
| Groups (section 3.7) | The members and owners you add yourself | Access to your personas and prompts; owners can edit them together |
| Sharing a chat (section 3.7) | UvA users with the link | A conversation to read |
| Embedding a persona in Canvas (section 3.5) | Students in your Canvas course | A persona to chat with |

ShareSquare is new and will be expanded step by step. This part of the manual will grow along with it.

## 5.2 Finding assets

### Explore

When you open ShareSquare, you start on **Explore**. Under the tab **All** you'll see two rows:

* **Popular this week:** assets that are popular at the moment.
* **Newest:** the most recently shared assets.

Each card shows the type and title of the asset, the author and their role, the publication date and a short description. Click the card to open the asset (section 5.3), or click **Copy to personas** (or prompts, projects, extensions) to copy it straight away (section 5.4).

Use the tabs **Personas**, **Prompts**, **Projects** and **Extensions** to see all shared assets of one type.

### Search

1. **Enter a search term:** type a word in the search field and press **Enter**. You can also click **Search** in the top bar first.
2. **View the results:** ShareSquare searches the titles and descriptions of all assets. The number of results appears above the list, with the newest assets at the top.
3. **Preview an asset:** click a result. On a wide screen, the list appears on the left and the preview on the right.

Under the tab **All** you search all types at once. Under a tab such as **Prompts**, you only search that type.

**Tip!** Switching tabs clears your search term. Choose the tab first, then search.

**Tip!** ShareSquare does not search author names. Search for words from the title or description instead.

## 5.3 Viewing an asset

When you open an asset, you'll see:

* **The title and type** of the asset, with the author, their role and their faculty or institution.
* **The publication date** and how many times other users have **saved** (copied) the asset.
* **About this persona** (or prompt, project, extension): the description written by the author, with the date of the last update.

Every asset has its own web address. To point a colleague to an asset, copy the link from the address bar of your browser. Click **Go back** to return to the overview.

**Good to know:** before you copy an asset, you only see its title and description, not the instructions of a persona or the text of a prompt. That is why a clear description matters when you share something yourself (section 5.7).

## 5.4 Copying an asset

Found something useful? Click **Copy to personas** (or **Copy to prompts**, **Copy to projects**, **Copy to extensions**). A message confirms that the asset has been copied, and the button changes to **Copied to personas**.

You'll find the copy in your own overview. A copied persona, for example, appears at the top of **My personas** on the Personas page, with "(copy)" after its name. The copy is yours: you are the owner and can edit it like any of your own personas (section 3.4).

**Tip!** Always check a copied asset before you use it in your teaching or research, and adapt it to your own situation. Give it a recognisable name, so you can tell it apart from the original.

## 5.5 Sharing your own asset

You can share your own personas, prompts, projects and extensions with other users. Click **+ Add** in the top bar and follow the four steps shown at the top of the screen:

1. **Select:** choose the type of asset you want to share: **Personas**, **Prompts**, **Projects** or **Extensions**. Then click **Next step**.
2. **Define:** choose the persona, prompt, project or extension you want to share. Use the search field, or click an item in the list of recent items. You then go straight to the next step.
3. **Details:** fill in the information other users will see.
    * **Publication title:** the name of your asset in ShareSquare. This field is not filled in automatically.
    * **About:** a description of what the asset does, who it is for and how to use it.
    * **Publication date:** today by default. You can choose today or a later date.
    * **Language:** English or Dutch.
    * **Check your details:** your name (**Author**) and your **Faculty** are filled in automatically. Choose your **Role**: Student, Teacher, TA or Employee.

    The title and the description are required. Then click **Next step**.
4. **Review:** check the preview. This is how other users will see your asset. Click **Edit** to change something, or **Publish** to share the asset.

After publishing, you return to **Explore**, where your asset appears straight away under **Newest**.

**Tip!** Want to share something new? First create it on the Personas, Prompts, Projects or Extensions page, and then share it via **+ Add**.

## 5.6 Managing your uploads

Under **Your uploads** you'll find all the assets you have shared. Use the tabs to show one type only, and the search field to find a specific asset. Click **Name** or **Publication Date** to sort the list.

* **Editing:** click an asset to open it and choose **Edit**. Change the details and click **Save**. The **Edit** button only appears on your own assets.
* **Deleting:** click the trash icon <Icon name="Trash2" color="black" size={20} /> next to the asset and confirm with **Delete**. This removes the publication from ShareSquare. Deleting cannot be undone.

## 5.7 Sharing responsibly

Everything you share in ShareSquare can be viewed and copied by other UvA AI Chat users. Keep the following in mind:

* **Write a clear title and description.** Before copying, others only see the title and the description. Explain what the asset does, who it is for and how to use it.
* **Do not share personal or confidential information.** Check the instructions and any attached documents of your persona or project before you share it.
* **Respect copyright.** Only share documents and materials that you are allowed to share.
* **Check what you copy.** An asset made by someone else may not fit your context. Test it before you use it in your teaching or research.

For the general guidelines on using AI responsibly, see section 1.1.
